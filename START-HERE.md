# EASIEST WAY: get the APK without installing anything big (GitHub builds it for you)

1. Open github.com on your laptop. Sign up or log in.
2. Click the "+" at top right, then "New repository". Name it HighwayScout. Click "Create repository".
3. Click "uploading an existing file".
4. Open this HighwayScout folder on your laptop. Select ALL files and folders inside it
   (including the hidden ".github" folder) and drag them into the GitHub page.
   If the ".github" folder is not visible: in Windows File Explorer click View > Show > Hidden items.
5. Click "Commit changes".
6. Click the "Actions" tab. Wait 5-8 minutes until you see a green tick.
7. Click the finished run, scroll down to "Artifacts", click "HighwayScout-APK". A zip downloads.
8. Unzip it. You get app-debug.apk.
9. Send app-debug.apk to your phones (WhatsApp / USB / Drive). Tap it to install.
   If the phone says "blocked", tap Settings and allow "Install unknown apps" for that app.

If step 6 shows a RED cross, click it, copy the red error text and send it to me.
